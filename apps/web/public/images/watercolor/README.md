# Watercolor pigment

`pigment.jpg` is a grayscale density texture generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the approved watercolor listening-wave concept (September 26, 2026). It was resized to 1024px wide and encoded as JPEG for delivery. Color and motion are applied by `src/lib/watercolor-paint.ts`; the image contains no interface text or baked-in wave shape. Cached blue, violet, and rose washes drift independently and bend in narrow, seam-aligned strips, so the pigment moves inside the wave as well as along its edge. The existing animation clock controls all movement, including settling on mute and stopping for reduced motion.

Generation prompt:

> Create a grayscale watercolor pigment texture map for use inside an animated web UI. Asset only, no mockup, text, labels, frame, objects or landscape. Landscape aspect ratio about 3:2, high detail. Fill the ENTIRE image edge to edge with a continuous pale-to-medium graphite-gray watercolor wash on clean white hot-press paper. Fine organic watercolor granulation, naturally irregular pigment pooling, soft cauliflower blooms, translucent overlapping washes, delicate darker tide lines surrounding larger cloud-like diffuse lighter regions. Multiscale mottling: many tiny grain details plus medium soft billows and a few broad wash regions. Accurate natural wet-on-wet watercolor texture, very fine paper grain. The gray varies mostly between white and medium gray, a few small dark-gray pigment seams. Even coverage across the whole image, no central subject, no central vignette, no blank border, no waves or horizon or silhouette, no brush strokes, no drips or splatter, no shadows, no blue or other color. Keep the broad variations softly flowing horizontally, with substantial randomness; avoid looking like rocks, marble, concrete or smoke. This will become a reusable pigment-density mask tintable pale blue/lavender/rose on a cream app background, so prioritize convincing delicate paint structure and feathered watercolor edges throughout.
