# Chitchat

Chitchat helps learners practice spoken small talk with a fictional conversation partner.

## Language

**Free talk**: A natural spoken conversation in which the learner composes their own replies and can request optional reply suggestions.

**Guided**: A conversation in which the learner follows prepared replies aloud. Its recap counts completed and skipped phrases rather than scoring independent composition.

**Reply suggestion**: An optional prepared answer shown during Free talk. It is not part of the conversation unless the learner speaks.
_Avoid_: Submitted reply

**Assistance shown**: The learner saw a reply suggestion for the partner turn preceding their response. This does not establish that the learner used the suggestion or composed the response independently.
_Avoid_: Assistance used

**Partner turn**: A spoken contribution from the selected fictional conversation partner.

**Learner turn**: A spoken contribution from the person practicing. Caption revisions describe the same contribution rather than additional turns.
